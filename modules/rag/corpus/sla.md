# Service Level Agreement

## Uptime
Enterprise tier commits to **99.95% monthly availability**, measured at the edge, excluding
scheduled maintenance windows announced 72 hours ahead.

## Credits
| Availability | Credit |
|---|---|
| 99.0–99.95% | 10% of monthly fee |
| 95.0–99.0% | 25% of monthly fee |
| < 95.0% | 50% of monthly fee |

Credits are applied automatically; customers need not claim them.

## Support response
- P1 (service down): 15 minutes, 24x7
- P2 (major feature impaired): 2 hours, business hours
- P3 (minor): 1 business day
