# Retention Playbook

## Risk bands
- **Critical (>= 75%)**: save desk within 24 hours. Contract buyout or 6-month discounted term.
- **High (>= 50%)**: targeted discount plus a proactive support check-in the same week.
- **Elevated (>= 25%)**: nurture segment, re-score in 30 days.
- **Low (< 25%)**: exclude from retention spend to protect margin.

## Approved concessions
| Lever | Cap | Approval |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly discount | 20% for 6 months | Agent |
| Monthly discount | 35% for 12 months | Team lead |
| Contract buyout | $2,500 | Retentions desk |
| Hardware credit | $400 | Team lead |

## What we do not do
We never discount for customers whose only risk driver is price-shopping with fewer than two
service calls in 90 days — the data shows those customers churn at the base rate regardless.
