# Refund Policy

## Standard window
Customers may request a refund within **30 days** of the invoice date for any unused service
credit. Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5–7 business days.

## Pro-rated cancellations
Annual contracts cancelled mid-term are refunded pro-rata for whole remaining months. A
**cancellation fee of 15%** of the unused balance applies unless the cancellation is for cause.

## Non-refundable items
Setup fees, professional services hours already delivered, and hardware that has been
dispatched are non-refundable.

## Escalation
Refund requests disputed by the customer escalate to the Retentions desk at the second
refusal. Retentions may approve up to **$2,500** without regional sign-off.
